# Meterly — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

Meterly is a macOS menu bar utility made by **MARB DEV**. It shows your network speed, CPU and memory use, and can keep your Mac awake.

## The short version
**Meterly does not collect, store, send or share any personal data.**

## Details
- **No data collection.** Meterly has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- **No internet connections.** Meterly never connects to the internet or to any server. It only reads network byte counters that macOS already keeps, the same numbers Activity Monitor shows.
- **On-device only.** Speeds, CPU, memory and session totals are calculated on your Mac and kept in memory. They're discarded when you quit the app.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (for example, menu bar style or units) are saved locally in macOS's standard preferences store on your Mac.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, Meterly shows local notifications (for example, "Download finished"). They're created on your Mac and never sent anywhere.
- **What Meterly can't see.** It doesn't read the websites you visit, the content of your traffic, your Wi-Fi network name or your location.
- **Children.** Meterly collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
